Guidelines & Conduct

Terms of Service

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Welcome to FOB Recreational Centre. By visiting our venue or booking any of our seven sport zones, you agree to comply with these terms.

1. Bookings & Cancellations

Pitch and court slots are held upon staff confirmation. Rescheduling is permitted up to 4 hours prior to scheduled kickoff. In the event of extreme weather, credits are transferred to your next booking.

2. Equipment & Footwear

Metal cleats/studs are strictly prohibited on the 5-a-side pitch. Only turf trainers or rubber studs are allowed. Non-marking court sneakers are mandatory on the indoor basketball court.

3. Safety & Sportsmanship

FOB is dedicated to high-energy sports and good vibes. Reckless play, abusive conduct, or aggression will result in immediate removal from the premises without refund.

4. Viewing Centre Admissions

Viewing centre match admissions are granted per match on a first-come, first-served basis. FOB reserves the right to manage venue capacity for guest safety and comfort.
