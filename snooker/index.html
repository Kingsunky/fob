Snooker FAQ

Can I bring my own personal cue? +
Absolutely! Many regular players bring their own two-piece or three-piece custom cues and tips. We also provide balanced Ash cues in various weights.
Can drinks and food be served at the table? +
Yes! Every table has dedicated side drink stations so you can enjoy cold beers and finger food while keeping food and liquids safely off the cloth.
Can I walk in or should I book ahead? +
Walk-ins are accommodated whenever a table is open. On Friday and weekend evenings, locking your table in advance via WhatsApp is advised.
