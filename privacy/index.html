Data Protection

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026 • Compliant with the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA)

At FOB Recreational Centre ("FOB", "we", "us"), we believe in transparency, speed, and privacy. Our website does not require user accounts, passwords, or personal profiles.

1. Information We Collect

When you use our website, we do not store personal details on our servers. When you click "Lock it in", the website formats a booking template on your own device and opens your native WhatsApp application. Your booking details and phone number are handled directly within WhatsApp pursuant to Meta's privacy policy.

2. Device Location Data

If you tap "Use my location" on our Find Us page, your browser's Geolocation API calculates the approximate travel distance and time to FOB at 2, Lawani Ode Street, Alakara Bus Stop, Ashipa, Ayobo, Lagos. Your coordinates are used locally in your browser memory and are never stored, logged or tracked by FOB.

3. Cookies & Analytics

We use privacy-friendly, cookieless metrics to measure general page views and button interactions (such as WhatsApp taps). We do not use cross-site tracking cookies, advertising identifiers, or invasive telemetry.

4. Contact Us

If you have questions regarding your data or our policies under the Nigeria Data Protection Act, please contact our team at 2, Lawani Ode Street, Alakara Bus Stop, Ashipa, Ayobo, Lagos, or via phone / WhatsApp at +234 803 000 0362.
