Table Tennis FAQ

Can we play without bringing our own equipment? +
Yes! Every table booking includes premium carbon paddles and multiple tournament 3-star balls. You're also welcome to bring your personal custom paddle.
Do you organize table tennis tournaments? +
Yes, FOB hosts a monthly Lagos Table Tennis King of the Table tournament with cash prizes and medals. Enquire at the desk or follow our Instagram for registration dates.
Is the area air-conditioned? +
Yes, our indoor table tennis arena is fully air-conditioned with draft-free ventilation so ball flight remains 100% true.
