Gaming Lounge FAQ

Can I log into my own PlayStation or Xbox account? +
Yes! You can log in via quick QR code scanning to access your personal save games and cloud profiles. Our system wipes temporary user credentials automatically when your session ends.
Can we host our own FIFA or 2K tournament? +
Yes, our 4-player and 8-player pods are designed specifically for crew tournaments with bracket displays and winner prizes. Lock in a pod via WhatsApp.
Is internet connection stable for online matches? +
We maintain dedicated redundant enterprise fibre connections with sub-25ms ping to European and African gaming servers, backed by unbuffered solar inverter power.
