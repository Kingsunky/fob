FOB Website: Product Requirements Document (PRD)
Version 2.3 (Alcohol & Beer Decommissioning / Fresh Smoothies & Healthy Drinks Overhaul) | 21 October 2026 | Status: ready for client sign-off | Prepared by the FOB project team (Brand Strategy, Sports Creative Direction, Art Direction, Copy, UX/UI, Development, Production)
1. Summary
FOB is a premier sports and recreation centre located at 2, Lawani Ode Street, Alakara Bus Stop, Ashipa, Ayobo, Lagos, with seven zones: football pitch, football viewing centre, gym, basketball court, snooker, table tennis and gaming. This document defines the requirements for the FOB website: what it must do, how it must look and move, and every illustration, animation, broadcast graphic, and map navigation interface produced for it.
The website has one job: turn a mobile visitor into a person who walks through the door or sends a WhatsApp message. Bookings across zones run through WhatsApp, phone and walk-in. The viewing centre operates as a live stadium-grade 4K sports lounge featuring a 4K laser projection wall and 105dB stadium surround sound alongside an integrated smoothie bar with healthy drinks, shawarma, and live charcoal grills, first come first served. In Version 2.1, all online upcoming and live match fixture listings have been completely eliminated; users discover daily fixtures and match screening schedules directly upon arriving at the viewing centre or via WhatsApp inquiries, keeping the website lightweight and laser-focused on venue atmosphere and walk-in conversion. The quality bar the site is built to is in section 17, and the brand system it is built from is in section 18.
Product principles
	•	Sleek in structure, fun in moments. Layout, type and photography stay clean. Colour, copy, motion and small surprises carry the fun.
	•	Mobile first, data-light. Most visitors will arrive on a phone on mobile data. Zero fixture polling overhead.
	•	The next action is always visible: Lock it in, Explore Viewing, Call, or Directions, reinforced by a persistent mobile thumb-zone action dock.
	•	The O is the hero. It is the logo, the zone icon, the loader, the cursor and the interactive toy.
	•	Nothing hidden: venue setup, audio/visual specs, operating hours, fresh smoothie, healthy drinks & food menus, and turn-by-turn directions are always one tap away.
	•	Zero pricing friction: the site eliminates all pricing tables and payment barriers; availability and table reservations happen directly via WhatsApp.
	•	Stadium energy, broadcast precision: Visuals blend Lagos crew warmth with sports broadcast typography and floodlight contrast.
	•	Cinematic presentation, authentic branding: Every visual asset across hero headers, interactive zone tabs, and photo galleries is framed with anamorphic letterboxing, subtle lens vignetting, and a micro-HUD glassmorphic FOB tag celebrating the iconic volt O.
	•	Accessibility as a baseline: Built strictly to WCAG 2.2 AA with minimum 44px touch targets, outdoor daylight contrast compliance (>4.5:1), keyboard focus trapping in dialogs, and native iOS form zoom prevention.
	•	On-site match discovery: Daily match schedules, lineups, and channels are posted physically on boards at the viewing centre, driving spontaneous foot traffic and lively walk-in attendance.

What we deliberately do differently
	•	PocketApp sells a financial product and leans on trust badges. FOB sells an experience, so we lean on sports energy, 4K lounge atmosphere, and community culture.
	•	FOB carries seven zone colours, used only as small badges, icons and section headers.
	•	No pricing tables: all pricing has been removed to maximize conversion speed directly into WhatsApp conversations.
	•	On-site match schedule discovery: all match fixtures and screening lineups are posted physically on boards at the viewing centre, eliminating online fixture maintenance and driving walk-in patronage.

Colour rules
	•	One bright colour leads on any screen. The others support.
	•	Volt lime sits only on ink or blue, never on white.
	•	Text on orange is ink, not white (white on orange fails contrast for small text).
	•	Body text is near-black on off-white or off-white on ink.
	•	Zone colours are used only for badges, icons and section headers.
Typography (locked)
	•	Display: Outfit ExtraBold (800) for headlines and Outfit Bold (700) for sub-heads. Locked in the working session.
	•	Text: Plus Jakarta Sans Regular (400) and Bold (700) for body, live scores, fixtures and labels. Locked in the working session.
	•	Scoreboard & Match Telemetry: Tabular figures enabled (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums; letter-spacing: -0.04em; font-feature-settings: "tnum" 1;`) on live scores, countdown clocks, and match minutes for broadcast digital-scoreboard precision without font payload overhead.
	•	Four font files in total, self-hosted, subsetted to Latin, WOFF2 only, with a system fallback stack.
Shape and motion language
	•	Circles, pills and soft corners. Buttons are pills.
	•	Sports broadcast lower-thirds, live momentum shifts, and pulse tags. Things roll, bounce, pop, or flash with stadium urgency. Every animation is short and purposeful.
5. Audience and key journeys
Audiences
	•	The crew organiser (16-35): books for friends or a team, wants availability, time and a fast reply.
	•	The match-day fan: wants to know today's fixtures, top competition games, live scores, goal scorers, doors time and whether it is full.
	•	The solo newcomer: gym or gaming, wants to know what is here, what gear is provided and where it is.
	•	The event planner: birthdays, corporate outings, school and community tournaments.
Core journeys
	•	Crew organiser: Home, pick a zone, check availability and hours, tap Lock it in, WhatsApp opens with a pre-filled message.
	•	Match-day fan: Home, Today at FOB strip, check live score and goal scorers on card preview, See you there plus Directions, or Ask on WhatsApp.
	•	Solo newcomer: Home, zone page, Call or Directions.
	•	Event planner: Home, Events page, enquiry on WhatsApp with party type and group size.
WhatsApp message templates
	•	Zone enquiry: Hi FOB, I'd like to book [zone] on [date] at [time] for [number] people.

9. Non-functional requirements
Performance (mobile data first)
	•	Largest Contentful Paint 2.5 seconds or better on a mid-range Android phone over 4G.
	•	First load under 1.2 MB transferred, with JavaScript under 150 KB compressed.
	•	Zero background live-score polling overhead (< 1 KB total static fixture payload).
	•	Images in AVIF or WebP with responsive sizes; heroes under 150 KB on mobile.
	•	Fonts: two families maximum, WOFF2, subsetted, font-display swap.
Mobile-First Style Guide & Ergonomics
	•	Thumb-Zone Prioritization: Primary conversion actions (Lock In, Directions, Call) pinned within natural thumb reach via persistent bottom dock (.fob-mobile-dock) on mobile screens.
	•	Safe-Area Inset Support: Layout integrates `env(safe-area-inset-top)` and `env(safe-area-inset-bottom)` to account for mobile notches, dynamic islands, and home indicator gestures.
	•	Zero iOS Viewport Zoom Glitches: Form inputs and selects enforce a minimum 16px (1rem) font size, preventing iOS Safari from triggering unwanted auto-zoom upon tapping fields.
	•	Fluid Type Clamping: Typography smoothly scales from 320px ultra-compact phones (e.g., iPhone SE) up to large displays using fluid `clamp()` tokens, preventing awkward word breaks or horizontal overflow.
	•	Horizontal Scroll Immunity: Page wrappers, grid containers, and media elements strictly bounded by `max-width: 100%` and `overflow-x: hidden`.
Accessibility (WCAG 2.2 AA Compliance)
	•	Target Size (SC 2.5.8): All interactive targets (buttons, chips, tabs, form inputs, mode toggles, modal close icons) enforce a minimum touch target area of 44 by 44 CSS pixels.
	•	Daylight Outdoor Contrast (SC 1.4.3): Normal text tokens calibrated to exceed 4.5:1 against the dark stadium ground (#0E0E2C); primary button contrast (Volt on Ink) exceeds 14.8:1 (exceeding Level AAA).
	•	Keyboard Focus Trapping & Restoration: Lock It In modal sheet and mobile navigation drawer trap keyboard focus while active, restore focus to triggering element upon dismissal, and support Escape key navigation.
	•	Live Announcement Regions (SC 4.1.3): Schedule updates and GPS route calculations announce dynamically to screen readers via polite aria-live regions (`aria-live="polite"`).
	•	Semantic Navigation & Skip Link: Complete with `#main-content` skip link, `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls` bindings on mobile toggles, and valid semantic landmarks.
