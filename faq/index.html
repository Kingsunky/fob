FAQ

Everything you need to know about FOB, grouped by category.

General & Location

Where exactly is FOB located?+
We are situated at 2, Lawani Ode Street, Alakara Bus Stop, Ashipa, Ayobo, Lagos. We provide free secure on-site parking with perimeter lighting and 24/7 manned security.
What payment methods do you accept on site?+
We accept all Nigerian debit cards via POS terminals, bank transfers with instant verification, and cash.

Bookings & Reservations

How does booking work?+
Tap any "Lock it in" button on the site. Choose your zone, preferred day, time and crew size. WhatsApp will open with your enquiry message already formatted. Our staff will confirm your slot within ~5 minutes.
Can I cancel or reschedule my booking?+
Yes, pitch and court bookings can be rescheduled up to 4 hours prior to your scheduled kickoff time free of charge via WhatsApp.

Match-Day Viewing

How do match-day seating and tickets work?+
General seating for football matches is first come, first served—arrive at doors-open time to claim the best spots. For reserved private crew booths or derby matches, bookings are arranged directly through WhatsApp with no payment friction on the site.
When do doors open before matches?+
Doors open 90 minutes before scheduled kickoff for major matches and 60 minutes for regular league games. Seating is first come, first served.
