Football Pitch FAQ

Can we wear metal studs on the pitch? +
No, metal studs are strictly prohibited to protect the high-grade turf and player safety. Astroturf shoes (multi-studs), rubber studs or flat training sneakers are required.
Do you supply balls and bibs? +
Yes! Every pitch booking includes clean numbered bibs and match balls free of charge. You can also bring your own match gear if preferred.
What happens if it rains in Lagos? +
Our astroturf pitch is engineered with a subterranean rapid drainage system. Even during heavy Lagos downpours, water clears within 10 minutes, leaving a non-slick playing surface.
