Got Questions?

Frequently Asked

Everything you need to know before walking through our doors.

How does the football viewing centre work? +
We screen major football matches and sporting events on our 4K laser projection wall with stadium surround sound. Seating is open, walk-in, and first come, first served — simply come through, grab a seat, and order fresh fruit smoothies, healthy energy drinks, shawarma, and live charcoal grills. Daily fixtures are displayed on our boards at the centre.
Can I just walk in, or must I book beforehand? +
Walk-ins are always welcome for the football viewing centre, gym passes, open shootaround, and unreserved tables/gaming stations. For football pitches, tournament snooker and prime weekend slots, booking beforehand via WhatsApp is strongly recommended so your slot is guaranteed.
What are your opening hours and sessions? +
We operate two dedicated daily sessions Monday to Saturday: Morning Session (7:00 am – 11:00 am) and Evening Session (5:00 pm – 9:00 pm). On Sundays, the gym is closed, while our football pitch and basketball court operate exclusively for the Evening Session (5:00 pm – 9:00 pm).
What food, drinks and refreshments are available? +
Our on-site healthy smoothie bar and kitchen serves cold-pressed smoothies, fresh tropical juices, electrolyte hydration drinks, organic mocktails, protein shakes, and match-day favourites: shawarma, live charcoal grills, spicy chicken wings, goat meat asun, suya platters, small chops, burgers and popcorn.
Is there secure parking and where are you located? +
Yes, we have secure on-site parking with 24/7 security and CCTV. We are located at 2, Lawani Ode Street, Alakara Bus Stop, Ashipa, Ayobo, Lagos.
How do I book for a large group, birthday or corporate night? +
Simply tap our Events Page or send us a WhatsApp message with your estimated crew size and preferred date. Our event coordinator will reply within minutes to lock in your custom package.
