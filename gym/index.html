Gym FAQ

Do I have to sign up for a long-term contract? +
Never. We believe in zero friction. You can drop in with single-day passes or renew monthly passes whenever you want, with zero automated card deductions or lock-in contracts. Enquire directly on WhatsApp.
Are personal trainers available on site? +
Yes! Our certified strength and conditioning coaches are available for 1-on-1 sessions, HIIT circuits, and athletic programming. Enquire at the desk or via WhatsApp.
Are showers and changing rooms clean and secure? +
Spotless. Our changing rooms feature hot rainfall showers, high-pressure water, hair dryers, and complimentary digital pin lockers cleaned continuously throughout the day.
