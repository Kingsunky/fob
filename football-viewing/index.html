Viewing Centre FAQs

Do I need to reserve seats in advance? +
For regular league matches, standard seating is first come, first served. For private crew booths (seats 6) or derby matches, you can reserve by locking it in on WhatsApp.
What screen setup do you have? +
Our main wall features a massive 4K laser projection display plus four 75-inch secondary screens ensuring unobstructed sightlines from every seat in the lounge.
Can we book the viewing lounge exclusively for a private game? +
Yes! We host private watch parties for supporter clubs, corporate teams, and birthdays. Check our Events Page or reach out on WhatsApp.
